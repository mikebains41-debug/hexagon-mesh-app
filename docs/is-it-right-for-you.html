Is Hexagon Mesh right for your text?

A short guide for deciding whether to embed a large amount of public text on charging phones, for search, SEO, product catalogs or research.

The short answer

Hexagon Mesh suits big batches of public English text that can finish overnight and where a low price matters more than instant answers. It is not a place for private data, other languages, or work that must return in seconds.

Phones do not replace data centers. Hexagon Mesh is a low-cost option for one kind of job: large, patient batches of public text. If you need instant answers, private data or other models, use a data center service.

Good fit and poor fit

Good fit

  • A large public archive you want searchable, such as articles, documentation or papers
  • Public product descriptions for a catalog
  • Pages on your own site, for related-page links or SEO work
  • Research text where you want to compare or cluster documents
  • Jobs that can wait until morning
  • English text, up to 256 tokens per text

Poor fit

  • Anything personal or private: customer records, emails, internal documents
  • Text in languages other than English
  • Answers needed in real time, such as a live search box
  • Models other than all-MiniLM-L6-v2
  • Texts that need more than 256 tokens in one piece
  • Work that cannot tolerate a slow or uncertain finish time

The facts in one place

Price
$0.005 per million tokens. The first 1,000,000,000 tokens every month are free.
Model
all-MiniLM-L6-v2: 384 numbers per text, English only, up to 256 tokens per text
Checking
Every result is computed on two different phones and kept only when both agree (cosine similarity of at least 0.99). Anything that does not match runs again on other phones.
Your data
Public, non-personal text only. Client data is deleted after 7 days.
When phones work
Only while charging, at 80% battery or more, on Wi-Fi and below 38 °C
Access
A small pilot. The public API is not live yet. For now, email a link to your file to thehexamesh@gmail.com and the embeddings come back as a file.
Who runs it
Manmohan Bains, in Canada

What has been measured

Each number says whether it is a lab test or measured on the real network of three Samsung Galaxy phones.

Simple English Wikipedia real network
Three phones embedded all of it: 644,083 passages from 278,882 articles (67.9 million tokens), every result checked on a second phone. Run twice. The first run, in October 2026, took 4 hours 27 minutes (shown on the dataset card). The latest run took 3 hours 18 minutes (shown on the home page). Public dataset
arXiv abstracts real network
3,225,386 abstracts in 18 hours 37 minutes. Public dataset
Speed real network
116 passages a second with three phones, every passage checked twice
Chip speed lab test
On a Galaxy S25 Ultra with full-length texts, the phone's AI chip turns about 45,700 tokens a second into embeddings, matching a full-precision computer to 0.9992
Search quality
64.5 nDCG@10 on the SciFact benchmark (300 questions, 5,183 science abstracts), matching the published score for all-MiniLM-L6-v2

The lab number is for one phone's chip. The network number is for three phones with every result checked twice. They measure different things and should not be compared directly.

A rough cost, worked out

The Simple Wikipedia run was 67.9 million tokens. At $0.005 per million tokens that is about $0.34 (67.9 × 0.005). The monthly free allowance of 1,000,000,000 tokens is more than 14 times that size. This is arithmetic from the listed price, not an invoice, and your own token count will depend on your text.

A job this size falls inside the first 1 billion free tokens each month, so it would cost nothing.

Questions to ask before you start

  1. Is every text public and free of personal information?
  2. Is it English?
  3. Can the job wait overnight or longer?
  4. Are 256 tokens enough for each text, or can you split longer ones into pieces?
  5. Is all-MiniLM-L6-v2 the model you want? Its search quality on SciFact is above.

If you answered yes to all five, email thehexamesh@gmail.com with a link to your file and what you want to embed.
