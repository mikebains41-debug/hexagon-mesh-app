What would embedding your text cost?

A plain worked estimate for a company with a large pile of public English text. The price is $0.005 per million tokens, and the first 1,000,000,000 tokens every month are free.

Phones do not replace data centers. This is a low-cost option for large, patient batches of public text. These are arithmetic from the listed price, not invoices. The public API is not live yet; see the access note at the bottom.

Step 1: count your tokens

Price is per token, not per text. A token is a small piece of a word. The only real data point we have is the Simple English Wikipedia run: 644,083 passages came to 67.9 million tokens, which is roughly 105 tokens per passage on average (67.9 million divided by 644,083). Your text will differ, so count your own if you can.

Texts are cut off at 256 tokens, so no single text counts for more than 256.

Step 2: the arithmetic, for some sizes

Tokens inside the monthly 1 billion free allowance cost nothing. We read the price as applying to tokens above that allowance. If your job is larger than the free allowance, confirm the exact billing with us by email before you start.

What this does not include

  1. Only public, non-personal English text can be embedded. Client data is deleted after 7 days.
  2. The model is all-MiniLM-L6-v2: 384 numbers per text, up to 256 tokens per text.
  3. Your own costs afterwards, such as storing and searching the vectors, are not included.
  4. A data center service will be faster, and will handle private data and other models.

How to start

The public API is not live yet. For now, email a link to your file to thehexamesh@gmail.com and the embeddings come back as a file. Read the plain guide first to check your text is a good fit.
